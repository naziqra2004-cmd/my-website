WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU WITNESS A CRIME

Follow these standard operating police protocols immediately upon discovering cyber extortion, fraud, or data breaches.

01

Isolate & Contain

Disconnect infected or targeted computers from LAN cables, WiFi, and Bluetooth immediately to prevent malware lateral movement.

Status: Cut Wireless Links
02

Preserve Evidence

Status: Unedited Logs
